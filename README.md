VS Equations Per Day
Note: These equations are assuming the Alliance Duel Tech Tree is at 100% completion


Day 1: Radar Training
Total=(300s)+(25,000t)+([X/660]*2)+(6d)+(5000p)+([f/100]*40)+ ([i/100]*40)+ ([g/60]*40)+(2250C)
s=Stamina
t=Radar Tasks
X=Hero EXP
d-Drone Battle Data Point
P=Drone Part
f=Food
i=Iron
g=gold
C=Chip Chests
Note: Hero EXP is calculated per 660 spent, therefore the total amount X is divided by 660, then multiplied by 2


Day 2: Build Day
Total=(125s)+(25p)+(200,000T)+(150,000t)+(3000S)
s=Construction Speed-ups (per minute)
p=Building Power
T=UR Trade Trucks
t=UR Secret Tasks
S=Survivor Recruitments


Day 3: Research Day
Total=(125s)+(25p)+(600v)+(25,000t)+(2,200c1)+(6,600c2)+(20,000c3)+(60,000c4)+(180,000c5)+ (540,000c6)+(1,620,000c7)
s=Research Speed-ups (per minute)
p=Research Power
v=Valor Badges
t=Radar Tasks
c1-c7=Drone Component Chests (per level)


Day 4: Hero Day
Total=(3750H)+([X/660]*2)+(20,000U)+(7000S)+(2000R)+(20m)
H=Hero Recruitments
X=Hero EXP
U=UR Shards
S=SSR Shards
R=SR Shards
m=Skill Medals
Note: Hero EXP is calculated per 660 spent, therefore the total amount X is divided by 660, then multiplied by 2


Day 5: Training Day
Total=(25,000t)+(125s)+(25p)+(25N*[L+1])
t=Radar Tasks
s=Speed Ups (Any kind, per minute)
p=Power Gained (Building or Tech)
N=Number of Troops Trained
L=Level of Troop Trained
Ex. I trained 1000 T8 troops, 25(1000)*(8+1)=225,000 VS Points

Day 6: Buster Day
Total=(200,000T)+(150,000t)+(125s)+(12.5RK*[L+1])+(2.5K*[L+1])+(2D*[L+1])
T=UR Trade Truck
t=UR Secret Task
s=Speed Ups (any kind, per minute)
RK=Rival Unit Killed
K=Unit Killed
D=Unit Lost
L=Unit Level
Ex. I killed 1000 T8 Rival Units – 12.5(1000)*(8+1) = 112,500 VS Points
Note: RK and K are different variables
